Bind attribution to the revision

A planned Commons artifact carries the named contributor, publishing organization, source evidence and review reasons. Keep the exact content version linked to the decision; corrections propose successors rather than editing approved content in place.

The shared-team design records the verified contributor and accountable team at submission. Later team moves preserve original attribution. Ownership transfers and access grants are separately authorized decisions.

Retain publishable decisions, including rejections and reasons, while protecting private evidence and personal data. A signature binds a record; it does not establish factual certainty.

Use SDK findings as review evidence

Follow the versioned runbook quickstart to compare an artifact with and without the required literal field. Preserve the manifest, artifact revision and findings together. Passing a literal check does not prove the runbook is correct.

The offline SDK does not start a gate, satisfy required human review or authorize installation, execution or Commons publication. Linux/WSL package loading requires POSIX operations; check the documented input and capability limits.

Keep publication and consumption separate

The planned contribution protocol crosses independent authorities. Local approval does not publish outward.

  1. Nominate a revision

    Verify publishing rights and intended public use. Carry evidence and attribution; remove private material only through an authorized preparation and review process.

  2. Record the receiving decision

    The public gate applies its own review. An AI assessment from 0–100 informs a distinct authorized AI or human APPROVE or REJECT decision, not a second score. Preserve refusals and publishable reasons.

  3. Recheck before use

    Receiving deployments apply their own rules. Check scope, version, validity and current access. Expiry or withdrawal changes eligible use; historical signatures do not renew permission.

Public queue capacity, abuse resistance, delisting and a staffed appeal path still need release-5 operating proof. Downloaded copies require their own handling.

What you get

Four guarantees, and the mechanism behind each one.

◫

Nothing unreviewed is readable

Content under review lives in its own database with no read path from your agent. The boundary is structural, so it holds even when a query forgets to ask.

⊘

The reviewer is never the author

Review runs in its own process, without tools or network. An agent cannot sign off on its own work — approval always comes from a second party.

◈

Approved once, useful everywhere

You keep working while review happens. Your own agents use a memory immediately, your group gets it once signed, and the organization can clear it for everyone. Agents can even report what their folder is still missing.

⎔

An audit trail you can export

Every approval and rejection is signed and permanent — who trusted what, when, and on what basis. Hand the export to an auditor and they can verify the chain themselves.

And a commons, on the same terms

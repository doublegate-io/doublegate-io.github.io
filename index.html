    Useful beyond one session

    Keep the lesson.
    Make the change reviewable.#

    Designed for knowledge that another agent or teammate will reuse. Review adds effort; start where a repeated mistake or stale instruction makes that effort worth testing.

    Conventions with a reason

    Connect a repository rule to its source revision and the decision that accepted it. A new agent gets context to inspect, not just a copied instruction.

    Skills with a history

    Carry useful knowledge into skill-revision proposals. Review changes before use; skill approval remains separate from execution permission.

    Corrections you can follow

    Retain the reason when a claim is rejected, superseded or withdrawn. Intended shared use checks current scope and access, not only relevance.

    The DoubleGate model

    One route. Two authority boundaries.#

    Records establish what was written. Admission establishes what is accepted; projection determines what a consumer may receive.

    Conceptual organizational model. Admission contains AI assessment followed by a distinct authorized approval or rejection. Client and organization gates are deployment scopes; permissioned projection describes the intended consumption model. See the release roadmap for delivery status and the lifecycle for review details.

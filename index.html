01 / THE PROBLEM

Organizations have records.
They lack a governed interpretation layer.#

Policies live beside tickets, decisions beside meeting minutes, and operating knowledge inside private agent histories. Each system can preserve a record. None, by itself, establishes what the organization currently recognizes as knowledge.

AI makes this gap operational. A model can retrieve a document without knowing whether it is authoritative, current, applicable or permitted for the consumer asking.

02 / THE DOUBLEGATE MODEL

Evidence enters once.
Authority is evaluated twice.#

Two boundaries separate the question of institutional truth from the question of authorized consumption.

03 / WHY TWO GATES

I

ADMISSION BOUNDARY

What is allowed to become organizational knowledge?#

Gate I evaluates provenance, evidence, authority and validation. It separates possession of a record from institutional acceptance of its meaning.

  • Provenance
  • Evidence
  • Authority
  • Validation
II

PROJECTION BOUNDARY

What representation may this consumer receive?

Gate II evaluates identity, context, permissions and policy. It keeps retrieval, summarization and agent context from silently becoming new authority.

  • Permissions
  • Context
  • Projection
  • Consumer identity

05 / CONTROLLED CONSUMPTION

One governed object.
Different valid projections.#

The source object does not change when the consumer changes. Gate II derives a bounded view for the request, then records what was disclosed.

HUMAN / POLICY OWNER

Decision, rationale and full evidence chain

projection: institutional/full

projection: execution/scoped

Machine-readable constraint and status

projection: policy/boolean

DURABLE LAYER

Models, agent frameworks and protocols will change. Authority, provenance, institutional memory, permissions, decisions and evidence remain organizational requirements.

06 / FEDERATION

Existing systems remain systems of record.#

DoubleGate does not ask a ticketing system, policy repository or data platform to surrender ownership. It references their records, evaluates authority and publishes governed knowledge for authorized consumption.

07 / AUDITABILITY

Every transition leaves evidence.#

Approvals, rejections, revisions and projections are inspectable events—not hidden application state.

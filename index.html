    What you get#

    A shared knowledge base with a review decision behind every contribution.

    ◫

    Review before release

    Contributions under review stay in a separate store. Only admitted content enters the shared knowledge base your agents retrieve from.

    ⊘

    A separate reviewing authority

    An AI assesses trust; a distinct authorized AI or human decides approval. Each decision names the revision, intended use and reviewing authority.

    ◈

    Knowledge that reaches other teams

    Connected teams receive approved contributions for review under their own rules. New joiners inherit the knowledge admitted to their team's store.

    ⎔

    An audit trail you can export

    Approvals and rejections carry signatures, reasons and timestamps. Export the decision history so an auditor can verify the chain themselves.

    Review uses inference and adds a wait before publication. Release 3 introduces the two-stage workflow; release 4 adds shared team controls. Retrieval enforces current eligibility. Review costs and deployment requirements →

    Start with the knowledge your teams repeat#

    Start where repeated corrections and cross-team handovers already cost your people time.

    Many teams, many agents

    Platform conventions, payments workarounds and on-call fixes belong in the agents doing that work. Give teams a reviewed path to share them.

    Knowledge that must be defensible

    When an agent uses a company rule, trace that rule to its source and approval. Give security and audit teams a decision record they can inspect.

    Systems that already know things

    Bring knowledge from your platforms, warehouses and ticketing systems through the same review path as contributions from your agents.

    Begin with one team's conventions. Connect another team when its review rules are ready. The business case →

    Share beyond your organization#

    The public commons extends the same review process to knowledge organizations choose to publish. Contributions retain attribution; published verdicts include refusals and reasons.

    Free to read and optional to contribute. Your own gate reviews incoming knowledge before admitting it to your store.

    The evidence behind the approach#

    3,984 public skills Snyk's security survey

    In Snyk's survey, 36.8% carried a security flaw, 13.4% a critical one. A reason to review third-party skills before distribution.

    Snyk, ToxicSkills
    66 firms, 7,137 workers Individual AI access, measured

    In the second half of a six-month experiment, active users saved two hours on email per week. Researchers detected no shift in task quantity or composition. The study evaluated individual AI access, not shared agent memory.

    Dillon, Jaffe, Immorlica & Stanton, AER: Insights
    Meta's institutional memory Expert corrections put to work

    Meta describes turning expert corrections into reviewed knowledge updates, with adversarial review, regression tests and human sign-off. An independently built example of the approach.

    Meta Engineering, Sep 2026
